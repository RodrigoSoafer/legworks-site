What LegWork collects, and what it does with it

LegWork is run by Rodrigo Soares. This page says what happens to the information you give LegWork. If anything here is unclear, email hello@legworks.com.au.

What this website collects

Nothing on its own. There is no analytics, no tracking pixel and no cookie set by this site. The fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your browser's request like any other web font.

What you give LegWork when you contact it

The form on the front page does not send anything to a server. It opens your own email app with your answers filled in, and you choose whether to send that email. When you do, LegWork receives what you wrote: your name, your business, your email, your phone if you gave it, and your answers.

What LegWork does with it

  • It is used to reply to you and to prepare for the call you asked for.

Your rights

You can ask what LegWork holds about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Email hello@legworks.com.au. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
